Security & Readiness

Security & Business Value

Secure by Design

  • Role-based access control across five roles — Admin, Manager, Operator, Auditor, API User.
  • JWT access tokens (15-minute) with refresh.
  • Multi-factor authentication.
  • Encryption at rest and in transit, with audit logging.

JWT + MFA
RBAC

Designed goals — 99.99% uptime, 10,000 requests/second, and sub-100ms p95 latency are stated targets, not delivered results.
